How it works

  1. Download

    Every weekday evening a GitHub Action downloads five years of daily prices for WTI, Brent, natural gas, the US dollar, the S&P 500, the VIX and 10-year Treasury yields from Yahoo Finance.

  2. Build features

    Prices become signals: recent returns, volatility, RSI, MACD, distance from moving averages, the Brent–WTI spread and moves in related markets.

  3. Train and test

    Ridge regression, XGBoost and LightGBM predict the percentage change 1 to 7 days ahead. Their average is shrunk toward "no change" as much as validation data says it should be, then scored on held-out recent data.

  4. Publish

    The results are saved to forecast.json and committed to GitHub, and Vercel redeploys this page automatically.
